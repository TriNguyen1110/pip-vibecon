# YC Requests for Startups (Fall 2026) — what matters for Pip
Source: https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs (read 2026-10-24). 13 requests; one is education.

| RFS | Partner | What they say | Pip angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Primer | Andrew Miklas | Adaptive AI tutor for young kids (read, write, arithmetic) at private-tutor quality, consumer scale; "Not a replacement for teachers, but a supplement that makes them more effective." "The best education has always come from one-on-one tutoring." | Pip is a Primer that reaches kids through the tutors parents already pay; it makes the tutor more effective (one tutor, four kids). Same engine extends down to K-5 arithmetic/reading. |
| Multiplayer AI | Aaron Epstein | AI is single-player; want shared live agent sessions people can watch, redirect, hand off. | Pip is one agent shared by kid, tutor, parent: the tutor watches what the kid taught, redirects (re-teach / whisper), and a new tutor picks up the student from Pip's memory. |
| Consumer AI for 1B people | Raphael Schaad | "Intelligence just got good enough: you can treat an agent like a person"; token cost falling ~10x/yr; learning is a named area. | Kids treat Pip like a classmate; falling costs make $4/student/month work. |
| OS for the physical world | Charlie Warren | Labor spend is 10–100x software spend; "incumbents charge for human coordination and visibility"; end-to-end work data is the edge. | A tutoring center is a labor business. Pip turns tutor hours into leverage (hours saved, renewals) and records what every kid actually understands — data no LMS has. |
