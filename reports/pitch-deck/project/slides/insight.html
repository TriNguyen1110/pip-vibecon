THE FLIP

The fastest way to learn something is to teach it.

The protégé effect

Students who believe they’re teaching an agent put in more effort and learn more, most of all lower achievers. (Chase et al., 2009)

Tutoring works

Across randomized trials, tutoring lifts learning by about 0.3–0.4 standard deviations. (Nickow, Oreopoulos & Quan, NBER)

Here is the insight. Teaching is one of the strongest ways to learn: in Stanford’s teachable-agent studies, kids who thought they were teaching worked harder and learned more, and the gain was biggest for lower achievers. And tutoring itself is among the best-evidenced interventions: 0.37 standard deviations in the 2020 meta-analysis, about 0.29 in the updated version. Pip combines the two: the kid tutors an AI, inside a tutoring center.
