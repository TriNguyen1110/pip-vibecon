Learning by teaching isn’t a gimmick; classrooms already use it. A meta-analysis of 28 studies found that preparing to teach and then teaching beats studying alone, effect size 0.56, and the benefit lasts. The UK’s Education Endowment Foundation rates peer tutoring at about six extra months of progress a year, for the tutors as well as the tutees. Prompting kids to explain their reasoning has a reliable effect of 0.55. And when eighth graders taught a computer character, they worked harder and learned more, especially lower achievers. Pip turns that into something a kid can do every night.
