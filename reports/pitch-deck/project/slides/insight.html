Why this works. Learning by teaching beats studying alone, effect size 0.56 across 28 studies. Explaining is active recall, and in equal study time recall practice left 61 percent remembered a week later versus 40 percent for rereading. And specific messages home matter: weekly one-sentence teacher messages cut failure to earn credit from 15.8 to 9.3 percent. Those last two are school studies, not tutoring-center data, so we say so.
