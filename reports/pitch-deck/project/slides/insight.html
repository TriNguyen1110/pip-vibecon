Why students learn better this way: teaching Pip is active recall. A meta-analysis of 28 studies found learning by teaching beats plain studying, a medium-sized effect of about half a standard deviation. And recall practice beats rereading: 61 percent remembered after a week versus 40 percent, in a study of 180 college students. These back the approach; Pip’s own impact is what a pilot measures.
