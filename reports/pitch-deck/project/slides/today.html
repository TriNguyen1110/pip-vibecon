Here is how a center runs today. Progress lives in spreadsheets and the tutor’s head. One math-center owner writes on their own site that filling each child’s sheet takes 75 to 90 minutes a day on busy days. That is one center, not a statistic. The center software we checked, TutorBird, Teachworks, Oases, keeps notes, attendance and scores. School systems keep grades the tutor usually can’t see. AI tutors keep a chat. None of them describe keeping the kid’s own explanation or tracking misconceptions. That is the gap.
