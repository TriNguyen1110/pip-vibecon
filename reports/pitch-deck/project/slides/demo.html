Here’s how it fits together. The tutor teaches the lesson as they always do. Between sessions, the student recalls it by teaching Pip: active recall that feels like a game, with a pet that grows with them. Everything they teach becomes one shared Brain. The tutor gets a profile and learning map of each kid, adapts to how that kid learns, and has invoices handled. The parent tracks progress with real proof, pays by bank in one tap, and messages the tutor. Pip is the connector between all three.
