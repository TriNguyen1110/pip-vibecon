The chatbot plays the teacher and hands over answers. Kids practice less thinking, not more.

Only 28 percent of eighth graders are proficient in math, and 39 percent are below Basic (NAEP 2024). More than half of teens already use chatbots for schoolwork (Pew, February 2026). In a field experiment with about a thousand high schoolers, students who practiced with unguarded GPT-4 scored 17 percent lower once it was taken away (Bastani et al., PNAS 2025). The guarded tutor version mostly avoided the harm.
