Progress is tracked by hand and still invisible to families

“It takes us about 75-90 minutes daily to complete these sheets on busy days”

A math-center owner, on their center’s website

“…no specifics on our son.”

A parent, in a public review of a learning center

Qualitative evidence, not statistics: Mathnasium of Littleton website; PissedConsumer review. Links in research/PUBLIC_COMPLAINTS.md.

Two real voices, quoted word for word. A math-center owner writes that keeping each child’s progress sheet takes 75 to 90 minutes a day on busy days. A parent writes that they heard about other kids succeeding but got no specifics on their own son. These are individual accounts, not statistics, and we present them that way. They show the gap Pip targets: progress is tracked by hand, and families still can’t see it.
