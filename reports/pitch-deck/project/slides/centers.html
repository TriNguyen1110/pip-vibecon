Sources: Mathnasium of Littleton center website (one center, not a statistic); public review on PissedConsumer.

Our customer is the independent tutoring center. Today progress lives in spreadsheets and a tutor’s memory: one math-center owner writes that filling each child’s sheet takes 75 to 90 minutes a day on busy days. And parents still get vague updates. One wrote that they heard how many kids were succeeding, but no specifics on their son. Families who can’t see progress stop renewing, and every renewal is the center’s revenue.
