A tutoring center is a small labor business. A tutor often has three or four kids in the room, two hours a week each. Every renewal is a sale: parents pay out of pocket and stay when they see progress. And tutors, often college students, turn over every semester. The market is huge and fragmented: 176,000 tutoring and driving-school businesses, 18.9 billion dollars, no player over five percent (IBISWorld 2025). EdChoice estimates 15 to 20 percent of students get tutoring in a typical month. Say plainly that we still need a real owner quote.
