02 · PIP’S BRAIN

03 · BOSS DOWN

Pip fails, so the kid doesn’t

A wrong rule shows up as Pip’s mistake. The kid re-teaches, Pip passes, the boss is knocked out.

Grades 5–9 math and SAT today. Claude plays Pip and grades each explanation on a 4-level rubric.

Pip is an AI classmate that’s wrong on purpose. It believes a real misconception, and the kid has to change its mind with a why. Everything the kid teaches becomes Pip’s Brain, in their own words, and Pip’s quiz uses only that. If the kid taught something wrong, Pip fails, not the kid. They fix it, Pip passes, and the boss goes down. Today it covers grades 5 to 9 math and the SAT.
