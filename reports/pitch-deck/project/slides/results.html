Offline engine; personas built from patterns in TalkMoves, MathDial, Bridge and Eedi. Not yet tested with real students or the live model.

We wrote eighteen realistic student personas from real tutoring datasets: the kid who says idk, the sarcastic one, the one who forgets the half, ESL kids. Then we ran Pip through them in rounds and fixed the rules. Sessions within target went from 3 to 11 of 18, boss knockouts from 6 to 14, and Pip now reacts to what the kid said 68 percent of the time. Three personas were held back, and two of three now go well. Be honest: this is the offline engine on synthetic students, not a classroom study.
