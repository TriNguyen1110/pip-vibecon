No “learning style” labels: matching teaching to styles isn’t supported by evidence (Pashler et al. 2008). Pip records how each kid actually explains.

For the tutor, Pip replaces memory and spreadsheets with a map built from the kid’s own teaching. Every idea Maya taught Pip is a node: explained, already known, fixed after a re-teach, or not yet. Tap any node and you see her exact words. The profile beside it is built from her sessions: she explains with examples, a picture gets her unstuck, here is the mistake she fixed, and here is where to start next time. We deliberately don’t label kids as visual or auditory learners; that idea has been tested and doesn’t hold up. We record what the kid actually does.
