Teens stay honest when parents aren’t watching, so the tutor is the filter.

The tutor sees everything: what the kid taught, where they got stuck, “fixed after one re-teach”, and a parent report already drafted. Parents only see what the tutor sends, or what the kid chose to share. No chat logs and no minute-by-minute tracking. That’s deliberate: a teen who thinks their parent is reading along stops being honest, and Pip only works if they’re willing to be wrong out loud.
