The market right now, US only. In 2024, 45 percent of high-school seniors scored below Basic in math on the Nation’s Report Card, the largest share ever recorded. Families are already paying for help: about one in five K–12 students get tutoring in a typical month, and in October 2025 another 24 percent of parents said they were looking for tutoring or planning to soon. Those tutoring numbers are for K–12 overall; there’s no public secondary-only figure, and we say so.
