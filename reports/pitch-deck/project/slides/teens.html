BUILT FOR 11–15

Made for teens, not for little kids

A classmate with presence

An art-toy Pip, front and center, that listens while you type and talks back. Deadpan, never babyish.

Pick your vibe

Nine looks from soft pop to chrome mech. Chosen by taste, never guessed from gender.

No red marks

Pip is the one who’s wrong. The kid is the expert, and the boss only takes damage from real explanations.

Texts like they text

One-line replies, double texts welcome, ready in two to four messages. No “Great job!”

The student side is built for eleven to fifteen year olds, and we pushed hard against anything that reads elementary. Pip is an art-toy style classmate, front and center like Talking Tom, that leans in while you type. Kids pick their own look by taste, never by gender. And because Pip is the one who’s wrong, there are no red marks on the kid.
