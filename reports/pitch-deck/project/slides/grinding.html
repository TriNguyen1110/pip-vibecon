Kids will still do homework. What changes is the kind of practice. In equal study time, students who practiced recalling remembered 61 percent a week later versus 40 percent for rereaders, who read the text four times as often. Rereading and highlighting, the most common habits, are rated low value; practice testing is rated high. And more homework isn’t automatically better: for junior high, the benefit disappears past one to two hours a night. Pip is recall plus explaining, in two to four texts. We won’t claim a number of hours saved; the pilot will measure it.
