There’s no public churn benchmark for tutoring centers; centers know their own. A free 30-day pilot replaces our assumptions with measured numbers.

Pricing is four dollars per active student a month, 99 dollar minimum. A 120-student center pays 480 dollars. Break-even is one family’s tuition: in our demo center a 12-hour package is 660 dollars, so keeping a single family that would have left more than covers a month. Staff time is valued at the BLS median tutor wage, 19 dollars an hour, times the minutes saved, which is our assumption until a pilot measures it. We’re honest that no public churn benchmark exists for tutoring centers; each center knows its own, and Pip’s dashboard shows it live.
