We’re careful here. Four dollars per student is our proposed price, not a validated one. The case is a simple break-even: if the tuition from families who stay because they can see progress exceeds Pip’s cost, it pays for itself, and each owner can check that with their own tuition and enrollment. There’s no public churn benchmark for tutoring centers and we have no outcome data yet, so a free 30-day pilot measures renewals, parent replies, and tutor minutes per report. The numbers inside the demo app are synthetic sample data.
